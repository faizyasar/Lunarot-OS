Instruction Booklet

Sacred Pachinko — a possessed board, a skeleton behind the glass, tarot deciding your fate.

01 Seating the conduit

Take the cartridge from its tray and press it home. The board wakes on contact — there is no power switch, and there never was one.

02 Releasing a bead

  1. Drop a bead from the top of the board.
  2. Pegs kick it sideways; every other row gives it a random upward hop, so nothing falls straight.
  3. The bottom row kicks hardest and least predictably.
  4. Side walls launch the bead rather than cushion it. Expect chaos.
  5. A bead that touches nothing on the way down is refunded — an unearned draw is no draw.

03 The gates

Each gate is marked with a glowing suit symbol. Whichever gate takes the bead chooses the card, and the card chooses the reading. You do not get to appeal.

04 The arbiter

A skull sits behind the glass and watches. It reddens and grows angrier the higher your score climbs. Its tilt and shake are honest telemetry: if it lurches, something went wrong.

05 Score & victory

Score glows gold and should be read as a prize rather than a statistic. There is a real victory at the top of the ladder, and it arrives with a flash.

06 Failure

Game over hits with a flash and a shatter, the text stumbling in piece by piece. Restart flashes and pulls back before it returns you to the start.

07 Care of your conduit

Do not blow into the connector. Do not purge the registry mid-draw. Store away from direct sunlight and from anyone who would use it on your behalf.
